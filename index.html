⬇️ Rooftop Rainwater Inlet Pipe Freeboard / Settling Space (0.4 m depth)
Layer 1: Coarse River Sand (0.3 m) Traps fine silt & organic dust (Top Nylon Mesh)
Layer 2: Crushed Gravel / Pebbles (0.4 m) Size: 5 mm to 20 mm graded aggregate
Layer 3: Large Boulders / Stones (0.8 m+) Size: 40–80 mm | Percolates into native aquifer
Safety & Honesty Tip: Keep the recharge pit at least 1.5 meters (5 feet) away from your house foundation wall, and fill it with boulders at the bottom, gravel in the middle, and coarse sand on top as shown in the blueprint.
